How it's made

From the market to your jar

01

Picked fresh

Raw mangoes, fat garlic and thin-skinned lemons, bought at the market the same morning.

02

Sun-cured

Cut, salted and laid out in the sun so the flavour soaks right to the centre.

03

Tempered

Chilli, mustard and fenugreek ground by hand and bloomed in hot sesame oil.

04

Rested & sealed

Packed into glass jars and left to mature until it tastes exactly like home.
